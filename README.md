# My Prehistoric Girlfriend

A short visual novel / dating sim for a 2-week class game jam.

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Genre** | Romantic comedy / slice of life |
| **Setting** | A general university in modern-day Japan with a creative, arts-friendly campus culture |
| **Tone** | Classic Japanese dating sim with early-2000s anime and retro visual novel sensibilities |
| **Structure** | Linear story; player choices raise favorability with individual characters |
| **Endings** | Three romantic endings, plus non-confession endings for lower favorability |

The player romances one of three anthropomorphic dinosaur-girl love interests: a Velociraptor, a Stegosaurus, and a Tyrannosaurus Rex.

## Setting & Background

- The protagonist's parents are working abroad, so he lives on his own in the family home.
- **Velociraptor:** childhood friend and next-door neighbor. Their families know each other, and she's used to dropping by his house.
- **Stegosaurus:** same year as the protagonist; volunteers on the department student committee. A professor assigns the protagonist to help the committee, which puts them in regular contact.
- **T. rex:** an upperclassman, one year ahead.
- The protagonist's home becomes the group's main hangout.
- The girls don't need their own side relationships or subplots with each other. Their interactions center on the protagonist.

## Story Outline

1. **Introduction** – Establish the protagonist, campus, and daily life. Introduce the Velociraptor (visiting his home) and the Stegosaurus (committee work), and give them a reason to start spending time together.
2. **Meeting the T. rex** – Introduced separately from the others. *She* saves the *protagonist*, which shows her agency and hints at a softer side under her intimidating reputation.
3. **Group Activity** – Bring all four together. Build familiarity through everyday interactions and individual character events.
4. **Water Park Plans** – The group hears about a newly opened water park and decides to go. The girls need new swimsuits.
5. **Shopping Trip** – Swimsuit shopping at the mall. Major favorability opportunities with each girl, plus swimsuit visuals.
6. **Pool Day / Finale** – At the water park, the player's final choices decide who he spends time with. Depending on favorability, she may get a private moment with the protagonist before the group regroups.

## Favorability & Endings

Choices affect favorability, not the overall story path. Each girl has a threshold for her true ending.

| Favorability | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **High** | Private time with the chosen girl, ending in a confession |
| **Partial** | A brief private moment, but the day ends as a group outing. No confession |
| **None** | Possible hidden joke ending: a meteor wipes out the group |

## Characters

### 1. Velociraptor — The Childhood Friend
**Archetypes:** Genki, Tomboy, Childhood Friend

**Design**
- Shortest; lean and athletic
- Feather tufts on the head or wing-like structures (refs: Dokibird, Selen Tatsuki)
- Red high ponytail with slightly messy framing layers
- Sharp, slightly upturned amber eyes; noticeable fang
- Long, slim tail (possibly feathered)

**Personality**
- Competitive, energetic, and athletic
- Mischievous and playful; loves teasing the protagonist
- Totally comfortable around the protagonist after years of friendship
- Turns ordinary activities into competitions
- Surprisingly vulnerable once things become genuinely romantic

### 2. Stegosaurus — The Dandere
**Archetypes:** Dandere, Shy, Gentle

**Design**
- Average height; softer build
- Small plates emerging from the crown or sides of her head, or a plate-like bow
- Ash brown or dusty rose medium-to-long hair, with a braid over one (or both) shoulders
- Glasses; large, soft, rounded blue eyes; softer expressions
- Substantial plated tail with thagomizer spikes

**Personality**
- Quiet, gentle, and somewhat socially awkward
- Anxious around unfamiliar people
- Struggles to start conversations or express affection
- Opens up and gains confidence around people she trusts
- Has her own opinions, boundaries, and occasional stubbornness

**Romantic Dynamic:** A relationship built on gradually developing trust and comfort.\
**Character Development:** Learning to express herself, communicate her feelings, and step outside her comfort zone.

### 3. Tyrannosaurus Rex — The Gyaru Tsundere
**Archetypes:** Gyaru, Tsundere, Confident

**Design**
- Tallest; fuller build
- Short, backward-sweeping amber/caramel horns (ref: Yoclesh)
- Long, voluminous, layered blonde hair
- Confident, fashionable look
- Narrow, slightly upturned purple eyes; sultry and confident; sharp teeth
- Large, heavy tail

**Personality**
- Confident, outgoing, and fashionable
- Assertive and a bit bossy; takes charge and speaks her mind
- Can come off as intimidating or overwhelming
- Enjoys attention and isn't shy about expressing herself
- Struggles to admit vulnerability or that she genuinely cares

## Scope

- Keep the story linear; choices only affect favorability.
- No major branching paths or standalone character subplots.
- Focus on each girl's relationship with the protagonist.
- Use a small set of recurring locations and events.
- Prioritize the core romcom experience over extra mechanics.
